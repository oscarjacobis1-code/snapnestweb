COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers before you spend on technology.

Buying technology gets confusing fast. These are the questions Guyanese business owners ask us most often.

What does SnapNest Digital Solutions do?

We build practical digital systems for businesses in Guyana, including websites, booking systems, customer records, online ordering, POS-linked workflows, inventory tools, staff dashboards, reporting, automation and custom business software.

Is SnapNest only for large businesses?

No. We work from the actual business need. A small business may only need a professional website and a simple customer workflow, while a larger operation may need several connected modules.

How much does a website or business system cost?

It depends on scope. Our public pricing separates one-time implementation from ongoing hosting, maintenance and support. Simple setups start lower; booking, inventory, staff workflows, integrations and custom features increase the implementation work.

What is the Business Technology Readiness Tool?

It is a free SnapNest tool that asks how your business works, what you already have, what problem you want technology to solve, how many people and locations will use the system, and what capabilities you think you need. It then compares your selections with a practical recommendation and preliminary GYD estimates.

Can SnapNest build only a website?

Yes. A website can be a complete project on its own. We do not force a business into a bigger system if the website is the only thing it needs.

Can a website later grow into a booking, ordering or operations system?

Yes. We prefer to plan the foundation so useful features can be added in stages when the business is ready.

Does SnapNest build POS systems?

We build POS and counter-sales workflows as part of business systems. Hardware, payment terminals and third-party services are scoped separately where required.

Can SnapNest work with an existing website or system?

Yes. We can assess what already works and recommend whether to keep it, improve it, integrate with it or replace only the parts that are holding the business back.

Does SnapNest provide hosting and ongoing support?

Yes. Our public packages include hosting, maintenance and support. The level of support depends on the system and service agreement.

Does SnapNest use AI?

Yes, where it is useful. Examples include customer assistants, enquiry handling and workflow support. We do not add AI simply to make a project sound more advanced.

Where is SnapNest based?

SnapNest Digital Solutions is based in Georgetown, Guyana and serves Guyanese businesses.

How do I start?

You can use the free Business Technology Readiness Tool, review our services and pricing, or contact us directly on WhatsApp to explain how the business works.
